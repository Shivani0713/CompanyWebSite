Our Services

Aeonsky Power and Systems

(001)

Retro fitment And
System Upgrades

Upgrade your existing generator, compressor, or power infrastructure without a full replacement. Our retrofitment services include control panel modernization (analog-to-digital), engine ECU/governor upgrades, emissions compliance retrofits, and integration of new monitoring systems into legacy equipment extending asset life while meeting current regulatory standards.

(002)

ATS (Automatic Transfer Switch)
Supply & Integration

We supply and integrate ATS panels (sourced through certified third-party partners) tailored to your genset and load configuration, ensuring seamless transition between utility and standby power. Includes system design, panel selection, wiring integration, and commissioning support.

(003)

Load Bank
Testing Services

Resistive and reactive load bank testing (delivered via certified third-party providers) to validate generator performance under real-world load conditions critical for commissioning new installations, annual compliance checks, and pre-deployment verification.

(004)

Remote Monitoring
& Telemetry

Every Aeonsky system generators, air compressors, air dryers, microgrid solutions, and BESS can be equipped with remote monitoring and telemetry. Track real-time performance, fuel/energy consumption, fault alerts, and maintenance schedules from anywhere, enabling predictive maintenance and reduced downtime across your entire power fleet.

(005)

Installation &
Commissioning

End-to-end installation support for generators, compressors, and dryers site assessment, electrical hookup, control system setup, and full commissioning with performance testing before handover.

(006)

Preventive Maintenance
& AMC

Scheduled maintenance programs to keep your power and compressed-air systems running reliably covering inspections, filter/fluid changes, electrical checks, and performance reporting under flexible Annual Maintenance Contracts.

(007)

Technical Consultancy &
System Sizing

Not sure what capacity or configuration you need? Our engineering team helps with load analysis, genset/compressor sizing, and system design including guidance on hybrid microgrid and BESS integration for future-ready power setups.

(008)

Spare Parts &
Breakdown Support

Genuine spare parts availability and responsive breakdown support to minimize downtime across your fleet of generators, compressors, and dryers.
