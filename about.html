About company

Building Today
Through Innovative and Reliable
Power And Systems

Aeonsky Power and Systems is an engineering-driven manufacturing company powering industries across Canada from heavy-duty operations to everyday commercial and light-industrial applications.
Aeonsky is led by a multidisciplinary engineering team with deep expertise across electrical control systems and mechanical design, backed by a combined experience base of over three decades in heavy industry. We exist to close the gap between what industry demands and what most manufacturers are willing to deliver: equipment engineered with precision, backed by real technical ownership, and built to a Canadian standard of quality and reliability.
Downtime is never acceptable whether it's a critical plant process or a single commercial site. That's why our team combines deep electrical control expertise with robust mechanical design, so every unit we build performs reliably, wherever it's put to work.

What We Build

    We design and manufacture a full range of power and air systems for industrial, commercial, and light-duty applications across Canada:
  • Diesel Generator Sets:Engineered backup power solutions delivering seamless load acceptance and dependable uptime, from heavy industrial installations to commercial facilities.
  • Air Compressors:Efficient, durable compression systems built to perform across duty cycles ranging from light commercial use to continuous industrial operation.
  • Air Dryers:Advanced drying systems that deliver clean, moisture-free compressed air to protect pneumatic tools and automation infrastructure at any scale.
  • Microgrid Solutions:Integrated power systems combining diesel generation, battery energy storage, and renewable sources to deliver resilient, scalable energy for remote, industrial, and commercial sites engineered to CSA and UL 9540/9540A standards.
  • DG Set Retrofit & Modernization:Upgrade services for existing generator installations, including control system modernization, emissions compliance upgrades, and integration with hybrid or microgrid architectures extending asset life while improving efficiency and reliability.
